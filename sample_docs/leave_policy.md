# Employee Leave Policy

Full-time employees receive 24 days of paid annual leave per calendar year. Unused leave of up to
10 days can be carried forward to the next year; anything above that expires on 31 March.

Sick leave is separate from annual leave. Employees get 12 days of paid sick leave a year, and a
medical certificate is required for absences longer than 3 consecutive days.

Parental leave is 26 weeks for the primary caregiver and 4 weeks for the secondary caregiver. It
can be taken in one block or split into two blocks within the first 12 months.

Leave requests must be submitted in the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except in
emergencies. The line manager approves or declines the request within 2 working days.
